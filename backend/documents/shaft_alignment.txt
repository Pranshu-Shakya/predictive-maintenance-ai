SHAFT AND COUPLING ALIGNMENT GUIDE

Proper shaft alignment is important for reliable operation of rotating machinery.

Misalignment can cause excessive vibration, bearing loading, coupling damage, shaft damage, seal failure, and increased energy consumption.

Common symptoms of shaft misalignment include excessive vibration, abnormal bearing temperature, coupling wear, repeated bearing failure, and unusual noise.

Angular misalignment occurs when two shafts are at an angle to each other. Parallel or offset misalignment occurs when the shaft centerlines are parallel but displaced.

When misalignment is suspected, inspect coupling condition, measure shaft alignment, inspect bearings, and verify that the machine foundation is stable.

Correct alignment should be verified after installation and after major maintenance activities.

Laser alignment tools or dial indicators may be used to measure shaft alignment.
