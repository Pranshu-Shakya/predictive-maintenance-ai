SHAFT MISALIGNMENT AND PUMP ALIGNMENT

Shaft misalignment occurs when connected rotating shafts are not
properly aligned. In pump systems, misalignment can increase
vibration and impose additional loads on bearings, couplings and
shafts.

COMMON SYMPTOMS

Possible symptoms include:

- Increased vibration
- Abnormal coupling temperature
- Bearing temperature increase
- Premature bearing failure
- Coupling wear
- Seal problems
- Unusual mechanical noise

COMMON CAUSES

Possible causes include:

- Incorrect installation
- Foundation movement
- Thermal growth
- Soft foot
- Improper coupling installation
- Shaft deformation
- Incorrect machine positioning

INSPECTION

When misalignment is suspected:

- Measure vibration.
- Inspect the coupling.
- Check shaft and coupling condition.
- Check machine mounting.
- Check for soft foot.
- Measure shaft alignment using an appropriate alignment method.
- Consider thermal operating conditions where relevant.

CORRECTIVE ACTIONS

Possible actions include:

- Correct shaft alignment.
- Correct soft foot conditions.
- Inspect and correct coupling problems.
- Verify machine mounting.
- Recheck alignment after corrective work.
- Verify vibration after the equipment is returned to operation.

EFFECTS OF MISALIGNMENT

Persistent misalignment can increase loads on bearings and
couplings and may reduce component service life.

SAFETY

The rotating equipment should be isolated before performing
alignment or coupling inspection. Follow applicable lockout and
maintenance procedures.
