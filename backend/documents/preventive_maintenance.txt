PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE FOR ROTATING EQUIPMENT

Preventive maintenance involves planned inspection, monitoring and
servicing intended to reduce the probability of unexpected equipment
failure.

CONDITION MONITORING

Useful condition-monitoring parameters include:

- Vibration
- Temperature
- Pressure
- Flow rate
- Rotational speed
- Operating hours
- Lubricant condition

VIBRATION MONITORING

Vibration trends can provide information about developing mechanical
problems. Increasing vibration may be associated with conditions
such as bearing deterioration, imbalance, misalignment, looseness
or hydraulic problems.

TEMPERATURE MONITORING

Temperature trends can help identify abnormal friction, lubrication
problems, bearing problems or other abnormal operating conditions.

PRESSURE AND FLOW MONITORING

Pressure and flow measurements help identify hydraulic problems.
Changes may indicate restrictions, cavitation-related conditions,
pump performance problems or system changes.

TREND ANALYSIS

A single measurement should generally not be interpreted in
isolation. Comparing measurements with historical operating data
can help identify developing abnormalities.

MAINTENANCE WORKFLOW

A general maintenance workflow is:

1. Detect abnormal operating behavior.
2. Review sensor trends and operating conditions.
3. Identify possible causes.
4. Perform appropriate inspection.
5. Correct the underlying problem.
6. Verify equipment condition after maintenance.
7. Record the maintenance activity.

SAFETY

Maintenance procedures must follow the equipment manufacturer's
instructions and applicable plant safety procedures.
