MACHINE LUBRICATION GUIDE

Lubrication reduces friction, wear, and heat generation between moving surfaces.

Insufficient lubrication can cause increased friction, temperature rise, wear, vibration, and eventual component failure.

Excessive lubrication can also cause overheating and increased resistance in rotating components.

The correct lubricant type, viscosity, quantity, and lubrication interval should be selected according to the machine and bearing manufacturer's requirements.

When abnormal bearing temperature or vibration occurs, inspect the lubricant condition and quantity before replacing components.

Lubricants should be protected from contamination by dust, water, metal particles, and other foreign materials.

Lubrication schedules should be based on operating conditions, speed, load, temperature, and manufacturer recommendations.
