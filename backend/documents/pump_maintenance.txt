CENTRIFUGAL PUMP TROUBLESHOOTING AND MAINTENANCE

A centrifugal pump transfers mechanical energy from a rotating
impeller to a fluid. Important components include the impeller,
shaft, casing, bearings, mechanical seal and coupling.

IMPORTANT OPERATING PARAMETERS

Important parameters for monitoring include:

- Flow rate
- Suction pressure
- Discharge pressure
- Vibration
- Temperature
- Rotational speed
- Bearing condition
- Seal condition

COMMON PUMP PROBLEMS

Common problems include:

- Low flow
- Low discharge pressure
- Excessive vibration
- Excessive noise
- Bearing overheating
- Mechanical seal leakage
- Cavitation
- Shaft misalignment
- Impeller damage

TROUBLESHOOTING

When abnormal pump behavior occurs, consider:

- Pump operating point
- Suction conditions
- Discharge conditions
- Flow rate
- Pressure
- Rotational speed
- Vibration
- Bearing temperature
- Mechanical seal condition
- Alignment
- Impeller condition

VIBRATION

Increased vibration can have several possible causes, including:

- Bearing problems
- Misalignment
- Unbalance
- Cavitation
- Mechanical looseness
- Hydraulic problems

Vibration should therefore be evaluated together with other
operating measurements.

MAINTENANCE

Typical maintenance activities include:

- Checking bearings
- Checking lubrication
- Inspecting mechanical seals
- Checking alignment
- Inspecting couplings
- Checking suction and discharge conditions
- Inspecting impeller condition
- Monitoring vibration and temperature

SAFETY

Maintenance should only be performed after appropriate equipment
isolation, depressurization and other applicable safety procedures
have been completed.
